What we build

A finance app that explains, not shames.

FinTrack helps people see what is happening with their money and what to do next. The product is built around everyday workflows: adding transactions, importing CSVs, scanning receipts, setting budgets, tracking goals, spotting recurring payments, and asking Money Coach for practical guidance.

The goal is not to make finance feel complicated or overly polished. The goal is to make it usable on an ordinary Tuesday, when you want a quick answer and a clear next step.

We keep the product focused on the things that help users act: plain-language insights, simple controls, and honest feedback about spending patterns without panic or guilt.

How we work

Our principles

Privacy first

FinTrack does not need bank credentials to be useful. Users can add data manually, import CSV files, and stay in control of what enters the app.

Plain language

Financial software should not require a finance degree. We write product copy, insights, and support replies in direct everyday language.

Useful before flashy

Every feature should earn its place by helping people make a better decision, catch a pattern, or feel less lost about their money.
