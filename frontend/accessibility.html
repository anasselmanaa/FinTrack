Accessibility

FinTrack should work for everyone.

We are committed to making FinTrack usable by as many people as possible, including people who use assistive technologies, keyboard navigation, captions, zoom, high contrast settings, or screen readers.

Statement

Commitment Standards Accessibility Features Known Limitations Feedback

Our Commitment

FinTrack is built to help people understand their money with less stress and more clarity. Accessibility is part of that promise. We want the website and app experience to be perceivable, operable, understandable, and robust for users with different abilities and different ways of navigating software.

This statement applies to the FinTrack public website and web app experiences available through `fintrack.art` and local preview builds of the same pages.

Standards We Aim To Meet

Our goal is to substantially conform with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 Level AA. These guidelines cover areas such as keyboard access, text contrast, visible focus, readable structure, form labels, error messages, and compatibility with assistive technologies.

This page is an accessibility statement, not a legal guarantee. We review and improve the product over time as the app changes.

Accessibility Features

FinTrack is designed with practical accessibility in mind, including:

  • Semantic page structure with headings, links, buttons, forms, and labels where appropriate.
  • Keyboard-operable navigation and controls for core workflows.
  • Readable text sizes, responsive layouts, and support for browser zoom.
  • Color contrast targets suitable for normal and large text.
  • Clear support channels for reporting accessibility barriers.

Known Limitations

FinTrack is actively evolving. Some newer or experimental areas may need additional review, especially interactive charts, AI-generated content, file upload states, and third-party services such as checkout or email flows.

When we identify an accessibility issue, we prioritize fixes based on severity, user impact, and how often the affected workflow is used.

Feedback And Assistance

If you experience an accessibility barrier, need information in another format, or have trouble using any part of FinTrack, email us at help@fintrack.art.

For the fastest response, include the page URL, what you were trying to do, your browser/device, and any assistive technology you were using. You can also report defects to bugs@fintrack.art.
